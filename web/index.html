    Benchmark this browser

    Replays one million requests of balanced flow through two storage designs: this project's dense ladder with a flat hash index, and the textbook std::map with std::unordered_map. It's the same C++ as the native benchmark, here compiled to WebAssembly, running on one thread in a background worker.

    Native, on one Apple M2 Pro core: 56.2 M vs 18.1 M requests/s.
